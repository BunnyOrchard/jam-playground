Each Play is that one sound, dry, on its own, from the same engine the firmware runs. It plays here in the browser, not through a connected FM-1. Notes from a computer only edit the scale, and the remote mutes the unit, so this page cannot send the presets to the instrument.

The boxes stay in this browser. A low bass or an 808 kick can vanish on a small speaker. Tick “Can't hear it” when it is missing here and on the FM-1. FM piano, vibes, kalimba, harp, the upright, flute, tenor sax, muted trumpet, harmonica and Dusty are sampled and are in this list. A new song can choose them with the others. Knob 1 still walks the whole list, one step a turn. What does not fit is more notes sounding at the same time.
